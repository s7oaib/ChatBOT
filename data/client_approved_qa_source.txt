Why is the Minimum order 9 pieces 250g for some flavours



Let me try to explain why the minimum order quantity is 9 in a flavour.

1. I tailor make all sondesh based on orders I receive.

2. ⁠so when I cook, any chhana dough less than 250 grams becomes grainy even at the minimum heat level at my home gas stove. So the texture changes & becomes unpleasant.

3. ⁠in short, the texture becomes bad if I cook less than 250g at a time = 9 pcs. Hope you understand

4. ⁠hence requesting to place orders in multiples of 9 pcs per flavour
